01 / The vision

Tradition,
reimagined.

The New Maharaja is a limited-edition Diwali home and lifestyle collection that brings together Sabyasachi's royal Indian heritage and PRSH. Studio's contemporary streetwear aesthetic.

The idea is to take the richness of traditional Indian royal homes and reinterpret it for a younger generation that loves fashion, streetwear and statement interiors.

MaterialsVelvet, jacquard, dark wood, brass, satin, embroidered textiles and patterned ceramics.
PrintsRoyal Bengal tigers, leopards, vintage florals, Mughal-inspired borders, paisley and occasional zebra print.
Streetwear twistOversized proportions, unexpected animal-print combinations, bold PRSH. graphics, relaxed styling and limited-edition collectible objects.
02 / The thinking
Heritage isn't a museum piece.
It's a way of living.

Why rebranding?

The aim isn't to change Sabyasachi's identity, but to reinterpret its heritage through a younger, more contemporary perspective. By introducing streetwear influences into luxury homeware, the collaboration explores a different way of experiencing traditional Indian design.

What new do I bring to the brand?

Through PRSH. Studio, a younger, more experimental side to Sabyasachi's traditional luxury aesthetic. Instead of focusing on clothing, a home and lifestyle collection that combines Indian heritage with modern streetwear influences.

Bold animal prints, oversized designs, vintage furniture and unexpected everyday objects make traditional Indian luxury feel more playful, contemporary and appealing to a younger generation.

04 / The audience

Who it's for.

Young, design-conscious luxury consumers who appreciate Indian heritage but enjoy expressing their individuality through fashion, interiors and unconventional statement pieces.

22–40yrs
Age range

Design-conscious luxury consumers.

Lifestyle

Fashion-forward, creative, socially active and interested in unique home décor.

Interests

Luxury fashion, streetwear, vintage furniture, art, Indian craftsmanship and collectible objects.

Shopping behaviour

Values exclusivity, quality, craftsmanship and limited-edition collaborations over mass-produced products.

Why it appeals — it allows them to incorporate traditional Indian luxury into their everyday spaces in a bold, contemporary and unexpected way.
